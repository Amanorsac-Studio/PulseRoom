PulseRoom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

PulseRoom does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

In short:
  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage or analytics data is gathered.
  • Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.
  • There are no adverts and no third-party trackers.

Information we collect

None. PulseRoom does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal detail, and it does not create an account for you.

Audio files you choose

PulseRoom can work out the tempo of a song. When you pick an audio file, the app reads it only to measure its tempo. The analysis happens entirely on your device, in memory. The file is never uploaded, never copied elsewhere by the app, and nothing about it is retained after the calculation finishes.

Network access

PulseRoom works fully offline and does not need an internet connection to function. The app does not send data to us or to anyone else.

Settings stored on your device

Any values you enter, such as the project tempo, exist only while the app is running or in your device's local app storage. They stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

PulseRoom is a music-production tool intended for a general audience. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics software development kits and no social media integrations. No third party receives data from PulseRoom.

Permissions

PulseRoom asks only for access to a file you explicitly choose, and only at the moment you choose it, so that it can measure that file's tempo. It does not browse or index your library.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
